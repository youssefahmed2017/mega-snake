Getting started

MEGA SNAKE is a free, single-file download for Windows, macOS and Linux. Get it from the Releases page.

  • Windows: double-click. SmartScreen may warn since the app is unsigned: click “More info” → “Run anyway.”
  • macOS: unzip, drag MegaSnake.app into Applications (needed for auto-updates), then right-click → Open the first time.
  • Linux: chmod +x MegaSnake-Linux && ./MegaSnake-Linux
  • From source: pip install -r requirements.txt then python main.py.

The main menu has: Start Game, Mode, Skin, Multiplayer, Daily Quests, Shop, Settings, How to Play, Stats, Leaderboard, Achievements, Changelog, Check for Updates and Quit. The mouse works on every menu; the snake itself is keyboard or gamepad only.

Pick a map before any match, in every mode. Speed rises with your score: a new speed tier every 60 points, up to tier 5. Difficulty in Settings scales speed on top of that.

Food, combos & scoring

FoodEffect
Normal (red)+10 × combo, grow by 1. About 85% of food.
Golden+50, grow by 1 (about 3% of food; +100 during Gold Rush).
Speed (blue)+10 and a 4-second speed boost.
Shrink (purple)Lose 2 length and 5 points.

Combos: eat again within 2.6 seconds to chain a combo, up to x20. Normal food is worth 10 × your current combo. The combo drops back to 1 when the timer runs out.

Near misses: scraping past a wall, obstacle or your own tail triggers a short slow-mo (toggle in Settings) and counts towards achievements.

Portals spawn as pairs and warp your whole snake to the other ring. Power-ups, downer-ups and portals are rare and spawn per second, so they don’t depend on frame rate. Hardcore has no power-ups.

Chaos events

Daily and Roguelite throw a rare surprise at you about every 1.5–2 minutes, announced with a 3-second warning. Events are solo-only.

EventDurationWhat happens
Food Frenzy10sFood everywhere. Eat, eat, eat!
Gold Rush12sAll food is golden and worth double.
Blackout12sThe lights are out. Trust your head.
Mirror Mode8sControls reversed!
Hunter14sA hunter snake is after you.
Earthquake9sThe ground cracks and rubble drops. It’s cleared when the event ends.

In Daily the schedule is seeded by the date, so everyone sees the same events at the same moments.

Battle rivals & Coop

Battle adds two AI snakes that go after your food; Freeze stops them. Coop is local two-player on a shared board with a shared score, and a Revive power-up appears when one player is down.

Multiplayer

Up to 4 players. The host’s game is the only real simulation; everyone else sends inputs and draws what the host sends back.

  • Host LAN Game / Join LAN Game: the host picks a ruleset (Classic, Walls or Maze) and a map, then guests type the IP shown on the host’s screen.
  • Host Online Game / Join Online Game: the host gets a 5-character room code. Guests enter it, and a small relay server connects everyone. A player who drops keeps their seat for 20 seconds and can reconnect.
  • Chat: press T during a match.
  • Compatibility: builds from 1.9.2 onwards can play together; for the smoothest result everyone should be on 3.2.3 or newer.

Ghost replay, stats & leaderboard

Every mode remembers your best run and replays it as a faint ghost so you can see whether you’re ahead or behind (toggle in Settings). The Leaderboard is an arcade top-10 with 3-letter initials, and Stats shows your lifetime totals and bests.

Color Blind Mode switches food, power-up and downer-up colours to the Okabe-Ito palette. Bombs keep a ring and curses keep an X, so they never rely on colour alone.

Gamepad support

Xbox and PlayStation pads work through SDL’s standard mapping. D-pad or left stick moves, A confirms, B goes back, Start pauses. B never abandons a run. The menu footer hints switch to pad buttons automatically. Stick sensitivity and vibration are in Settings → Controller; the pad buzzes when you eat, die or are saved by a shield.

Auto-updater & saves

On launch the game checks GitHub Releases for a newer version and offers to download and install it (“Check for Updates” does the same on demand). itch.io builds turn this off because the store updates the game.

Progress (coins, achievements, leaderboard, settings) saves to .megasnake/save.json in your user folder; ghosts are in ghosts.json beside it.
